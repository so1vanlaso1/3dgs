# Preliminary RGB-depth edge measurements

Date: 2026-11-03. **Decision: alignment remains inconclusive; do not apply an offset or start the training branch on this evidence.** A small leftward depth shift appears on one statue silhouette, but the test does not isolate calibration or timing and does not cover enough independent structures and distances.

## Method and evidence

Reprojected 14 prepared frames from 64.979–69.016 s using the same unfilled, unsmoothed depth method as the [initial alignment audit](ROOM_RGBD_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT_20261103.md). [Motion contact sheet](../results/room_improve_alignment_motion_20261103/motion_contact.jpg) and individual depth maps show the same exhibit from nearby viewpoints. Depth is original sensor output, projected into prepared RGB coordinates; holes are not filled. All 14 runs reproduced the saved 16,000 cleaned-depth samples exactly in pixel keys and depths. Existing frame choices mean this is not an exhaustive raw-frame or time-offset search.

For the standing statue's head silhouette, manually selected narrow image regions in three development frames and two other frames designated as validation. Within each region and each horizontal scanline, `scripts/measure-room-rgbd-edges.py` picks the strongest horizontal Sobel intensity gradient and independently the strongest valid adjacent depth jump (threshold > max(6 cm, 2.5% of nearer depth)); it reports depth pixel x minus RGB pixel x. Negative means the chosen depth edge is to the left. Both RGB and depth choices are automated; the markers are **not manually annotated ground truth**. Thresholds and strips are exploratory. Depth discontinuity is assigned to the left sample of the pair; its geometric boundary is between pixels, creating roughly half-pixel convention uncertainty. RGB blur, quantization, hole patterns and mistaken edge correspondences add further uncertainty. Markers and all accepted and rejected scanlines are in [measurement artifacts](../results/room_improve_edge_measurements_v02_20261103/). Cyan denotes selected RGB gradient; magenta denotes selected depth jump.

## Independent-structure attempt and rejected measurements

Also tested the right side of the backdrop structure in the same five frames. In the [marker images](../results/room_improve_edge_measurements_v02_20261103/), automatic RGB and depth choices frequently land on different sides of the broad teal frame or on different foreground/background surfaces. One frame yields only one candidate row. Those offsets are **rejected as correspondence measurements**, despite their presence in the raw CSV and JSON; they do not validate or refute the head-silhouette shift. One low-motion view around 64.979 s lacks adequate depth discontinuities at the head, demonstrating another missing-evidence case. Reflective display cases and far structures were not used as reliable independent controls.

## Interpretation and next step

The development head medians are -2, -4, -2 pixels and the more usable held-out-frame head median is -2 pixels. This is a narrow, correlated observation of one structure at changing viewpoints, not evidence of a universal calibration offset, a physical depth error, or an established RGB-depth timing error. Validation frames share the same scene and capture trajectory. No proposed correction was fit or applied, so those frames have not validated a correction. There is no meaningful far-range measurement or independent direction of edge orientation here.

Next, build manually reviewed corresponding edges on multiple non-reflective objects at distinct distances and orientations, reject ambiguous depth/occlusion boundaries, and compare motion-matched observations on the same structures. Use separate frames and regions to evaluate any proposed calibration or timing candidate. If data cannot support this, report the affected regions as unresolved. No point deletion, pose change or training was performed.
