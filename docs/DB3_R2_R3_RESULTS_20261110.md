## R3: support, controlled policies and numerical repair

Both submaps initialize from 90,000 evidence-ranked representatives in 1 cm
voxels. Supported candidates need at least two separated agreeing fitting views,
no observed free-space conflict, and stability/incidence/motion/clipping checks.
Missing depth and occluded content remain unknown. Confidence is an evidence
model; this is not a verified opaque/dynamic classifier. Raw uncertain observations
remain saved in `dataset/evidence/` and the original recording. Thin/reflected or
missing-depth structures do not become deletion labels.

All three arms share fixed cameras, exact initialization, 7,000-update replay,
seed 42, 21 training identities, strategy and 180k cap. Depth arms share up to
4,096 valid fitting-tile samples per frame. Off is appearance-only optimization
on RGB-D-derived geometry. Current uses the historical unweighted disparity L1
policy; confidence normalizes positive evidence weights. This comparison does
not separately establish an initialization advantage.

The first unweighted matte run concealed 1,487 NaN Gaussians behind finite image
metrics. Actual checkpoint reload rejected it. `torch.where(pred>0,1/pred,0)`
evaluated zero reciprocals in an unselected branch, poisoning backward. Masked
inputs before reciprocal, preserving the finite-positive objective, then reran
the identical control in `current_safe/`. All its final parameters are finite.
The failed run and partial evaluation are retained. New recorded runs now stop
on non-finite final parameters instead of publishing success. The final relevant
suite passes **245 tests**, with one existing unknown pytest `env` warning.

**Original gates fail:** no arm reduces every target-view median depth error by
10 mm. Confidence also misses the original noninferiority gate versus repaired

Confidence is the only arm within 10 mm worsening of every checked matte target
and control. Current worsens one target by 11.08 mm; off worsens additional
controls. Confidence improves target inspection RGB PSNR by 3.805, 1.623 and
3.944 dB. Image-relative coarse regions do not track one physical object through
camera rotation; they are inspection patches, not physical metrology annotations.

Selected confidence before exhibit training for a narrower confirmation: target
appearance improves >=1 dB per checked view/ROI; every target/control median depth
error stays within 10 mm of baseline; alpha coverage stays within 0.02; global
appearance is noninferior; captured/short-offset structure review passes. Added
original-capture statue/post/wall/floor rectangles before reviewing exhibit
Gaussian metrics to check actual structures as well as coarse patches.

This is a new, documented follow-up after the failed depth-gain gate. A pass would
support local appearance defect reduction with sensor-depth consistency preserved,
not universal depth improvement, improved metric accuracy or confidence-policy
superiority. The selected policy remains fixed for exhibit confirmation.
