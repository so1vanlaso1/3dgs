# R4 quality review — reconstruction goal remains unmet

Date: 11 November 2026, Asia/Saigon.

**Verdict:** the user's concern is supported. R4 completed a bounded geometry
screen and packaged a fallback, but did not improve the whole-room model.
Completion of the experiment must not be used as completion of reconstruction.

## What was actually delivered

The delivery manifest points to the original
`results/realsense_20261030_134523_120k/train/ckpts/final_post_update.pt`.
The separately delivered matte checkpoint comes from R3 and is not globally
aligned with that room. R4 ran no new whole-scene training, global bundle
adjustment or model merge. The phase decision explicitly records these limits.

Fresh review verified all 50 sealed delivery artifacts and both selected model
hashes against the saved manifest. Their integrity is intact; that does not
establish reconstruction quality. The full raw-file and dense-depth audit was
not repeated in this review.

## Visible defects and appearance distribution

Reviewed the full-route contact sheet and the original-resolution captured/rendered
pairs at indices 204 and 389. At approximately 68 seconds, the exhibit remains
recognizable but figures, backdrop texture and fine boundaries are softened;
bright doorway detail is poorly reproduced. At approximately 130 seconds,
display-frame edges and distant room structure are visibly smeared, with loss
of the captured person's detail. The capture also contains blur, bright clipping
and a person; these comparisons do not isolate a unique physical cause.

## Why the native retries could not resolve most failures

The saved edge roster contains 132 passing and 185 failing eligible selected
pairs: a 41.6% local pair pass rate. Another 159 intervals were excluded by
reservations and are not measured failures. None of the 185 native retries
passed the complete gate.

**New finding:** 176/185 failed selected pairs (95.1%) have fewer than the
required 20 depth-supported check matches. In `db3_r4_route.py`, native retries
estimate a new chained transform, but the endpoint check repeats the original
endpoint descriptors, spatial masks, descriptor matching and depth filtering.
Those operations do not depend on the new transform. Therefore native chaining
cannot raise the endpoint match count for these 176 pairs under this recipe.
This is a structural limitation of this experiment, not proof of bad geometry.

The saved data confirms identical original/endpoint check counts in all 77
completed native chains. Of these, 72 fail the check-count requirement and five
have enough matches but fail the error limits. The other 108 retries stop on a
failed short link. Error gates can overlap check-count failures, so not every
count-limited pair would pass if more matches were supplied.

The five sufficiently supported endpoint failures have the following residuals:

| Boundary | Check matches | Median error | p90 error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 113 | 49 | 0.02164 m | 0.15120 m |
| 126 | 25 | 0.09552 m | 0.15976 m |
| 149 | 50 | 0.05345 m | 0.16967 m |
| 332 | 24 | 0.02174 m | 0.12130 m |
| 449 | 23 | 0.01893 m | 0.47636 m |

The frozen limits remain median <0.05 m and p90 <0.12 m. These residuals alone
cannot distinguish correspondence errors, depth errors and pose errors.

## Decision and concrete next experiment

Keep R4's whole-scene reconstruction milestone **unmet**. Its audit and fallback
package are useful research outputs, but they do not satisfy the requested
whole-room improvement. R5 can inspect viewer operation; it cannot repair this
scientific shortfall.

The next bounded experiment should return to pose/overlap recovery on one failed
transition with successful short links, alongside a passing control. Freeze
source IDs and checks before execution. Build persistent multi-frame tracks and
refine a local camera/landmark window; establish genuinely additional validation
support through overlapping views or independently reviewed static structures.
Keep fitting/check identities disjoint and retain all reservations. Compare
against the existing direct-pair and chained transforms on identical observations.
The new protocol must define how sufficient validation support is obtained,
rather than reusing a failed endpoint-count check or lowering its threshold.

Review the sufficiently supported but inaccurate endpoints separately, including
the large-tail boundary 449. Only after a bounded candidate improves supported
geometry and the fixed difficult structures should it advance to a local Gaussian
comparison and then route expansion. This is a proposed next experiment, not an
executed fix. The review does not establish that recapture is necessary or that
calibration, timing, motion or model capacity is the unique cause.
