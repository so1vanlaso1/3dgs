# Pipeline diagnosis: 20261030_134523 recording — 2026-11-02

## Question and scope

Identify whether the capture or processing explains the perceived poor Gaussian scene. This is an evidence review, not a controlled ablation or independently measured trajectory. Existing data and model were preserved.

## Evidence

- Capture has 4,764 RGB frames; 477 were selected at 3 FPS. Images show bright outdoor openings, glare and reflective display surfaces, with small exhibit details and people in some images. These can create depth holes, view-dependent appearance and inconsistent observations. Quality gates selected 477 with no rejected candidates, but that does not establish geometry quality or scene coverage.
- Valid-depth fraction percentiles reported by cleaning are 0.323, 0.703, 0.955 (see cleaning_report.json for percentile definition). Timestamp-pair gap percentiles 5.50, 7.24, 7.91, 8.93 ms. Samples pass numerical bounds, not independent geometric alignment validation.
- RGB-only reconstruction registered 301/477 (63.1%) in its best model and generated multiple fragments. RGB-D bridging produced 477/477 registered; this demonstrates graph connectivity, not trajectory or scene-surface correctness. 44 bridge boundaries accepted. Pose graph has 1734 input constraints, 1731 optimized, p90 translation residual 5.15 cm, p99 14.66 cm; p90 rotation 0.924 degrees, p99 2.585 degrees. These are residuals against fitted constraints, not ground truth.
- Edge spans: median 2 frame indices, p90 4, p99 8, max 114; 4 of 1734 span at least 30 indices and 3 span at least 90. Long-span edges include scale-gated SfM-fragment edges and should not automatically be interpreted as independently validated loop closures. Global drift is plausible but not established.
- At each training view, up to 1500 depth points are back-projected using its estimated pose; 2.5 cm voxel selection and a random 180000-point cap follow. This initial cloud does not require multi-view support. The separate depth screening found 2513 points with at least 3 free-space conflicts and no support in 60 sampled views, but sampled views, edges, occlusion, dynamics and pose errors limit interpretation. Some reviewed projections lie on or near exhibit objects and ropes; not safe ground truth for removal.
- The completed model's 60 jointly posed heldout views give PSNR 23.1133 dB, SSIM 0.79857, LPIPS 0.30898. This interpolation evaluation is not independent geometry accuracy or free-view quality. Comparison sheet shows convincing broad shape in some captured views, but blur/smearing and missed detail particularly near reflective/bright areas. No earlier matched checkpoint exists to evaluate whether extending training improved quality or caused overfitting.
- Training used 417 views, depth weight 0.01, 120000 updates, densification stopped at 6000, final 1063961 Gaussians. The chosen settings have no controlled comparison for this recording. No basis to attribute defects specifically to depth loss, number of steps or Gaussian count.

## Assessment and next test

Both input conditions and pipeline choices contribute plausible risks. Highest-priority unverified risk is globally inaccurate poses and the pipeline gate accepting connectivity plus in-sample edge residuals without independent revisit/surface checks. Observed poor RGB registration and mostly local graph edges motivate testing; they do not establish actual drift. Unverified depth/pose errors can enter initialization and depth supervision; glass, glare, clipped highlights and incomplete capture are visible source limitations. 3 FPS uses 477 of 4764 frames; lower view density could hurt, but a higher-FPS comparison is needed to establish an effect. No quantitative blame split is supported.

Recommended sequence: identify static repeat views distributed around recording and compare held-out RGB feature and depth 3D alignment under estimated poses, including independent non-coplanar regions; inspect RGB-depth overlays and invalid/edge masks; then isolate an especially bad region with heldout views. Once poses and depth pass, run matched short ablations changing one factor at a time (same split, initialization and budget) for no-depth vs depth supervision, followed by a comparable higher-frame-rate run with separately validated poses and safe VRAM cap. Evaluate heldout images and consistent free-view artifacts; keep original as baseline. If capture has missing angles or specular/transparent surfaces, recapture may be required. Avoid deleting all red points or retraining 120k before the cause is measured.
