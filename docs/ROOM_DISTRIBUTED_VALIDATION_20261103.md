# SDK room candidate: distributed revisit and region review

Date: 2026-11-03. Decision: **geometry gate remains open**. No training, point deletion or pose correction was performed. This follow-up expands the existing evaluation; it does not independently measure physical accuracy.

## Distributed revisit search

`scripts/audit-room-sdk-distributed-revisits.py` applies the existing SIFT/3D revisit diagnostic to the 30 already projected unfilled-depth frames numbered 10, 26, ..., 474. It enumerates frame-index separations 32 to 192: 253 pairs. The frame list and separation were set before examining these results, but the same capture and most locations have already been inspected. Both pose graphs use the frames, and fitting a fundamental matrix on one split does not make the other split independent of the graph. Corrected-image features select the matches; no semantic or independent physical match validation is present. Repeated display motifs, reflections, moving visitors and planar features can defeat the test. The 3x3 stable-depth requirement excludes discontinuities and holes, but does not prove depth or alignment accuracy.

242 pairs had fewer than 25 mutual ratio matches; three further pairs had fewer than three jointly stable depth checks; eight produced diagnostic results. None of the eight is a direct stored graph edge. Check matches cover only 0.55%?6.74% of the first image by convex hull; they are not a spatially distributed room-wide constraint. [Contact sheet](../results/room_improve_sdk_distributed_revisits_20261103/contact_sheet.jpg), [full per-pair report](../results/room_improve_sdk_distributed_revisits_20261103/report.json), and individual match images are retained.

Four pairs had a lower candidate median and four a higher one. For 58/154 the old and candidate medians were 0.789/0.310 m across 12 points; matches are concentrated on display panels and similar repeated content, so the apparent gain is not accepted as a physical correction. For 202/250 they were 0.105/0.117 m across 34 points, concentrated on the exhibit backdrop. For 266/314 they were 0.046/0.048 m across 20 points. These point-level observations are correlated, and the numbers are neither independent error bounds nor proof that either graph is accurate. The contact sheet reveals the apparent range of pairs largely collapses onto a few repeated structures. This does not resolve the earlier geometry gate or provide a sound new pose constraint.

## Mesh, cloud and source review

Reviewed the existing [four-view dense mesh sheet](../results/room_improve_sdk_dense_test_mesh_20261103/captured_views_contact_sheet.jpg), [captured-view cloud sheet](../results/room_improve_sdk_cloud_views_20261103/captured_contact_sheet.jpg), and unfilled original depth overlays for [43 s](../results/room_improve_rgbd_alignment_20261103/frame_000129.png.comparison.jpg), [61 s](../results/room_improve_rgbd_alignment_20261103/frame_000183.png.comparison.jpg), [68 s](../results/room_improve_rgbd_alignment_20261103/frame_000204.png.comparison.jpg) and [110 s](../results/room_improve_rgbd_alignment_20261103/frame_000329.png.comparison.jpg). The mesh previews use approximate painter rendering and camera poses from the candidate; depth overlays use the original RGB mapping, so their contours are not a precise pixelwise check of candidate alignment.

* At 43 s, distant wall and ceiling areas are fragmented or missing in the mesh; the source depth overlay has broad unobserved regions there. Missing depth is not evidence that those surfaces are empty.
* At 61 s, the doorway/exhibit region is largely missing in the mesh preview and sparse in the cloud; the source depth also has a broad missing region over the distant scene. This supports retaining the region as uncertain, not filling or deleting it automatically.
* At 68 s, depth observations trace parts of the exhibit figures but include holes and boundaries; the mesh is fragmented and merges visual structures. These observations do not identify whether poses, depth, meshing or a combination caused every artifact.
* At 110 s, glass and reflections coincide with jagged, smeared mesh areas and uncertain depth. They cannot be interpreted as reliable physical surfaces from these views alone.

The source cloud is sparse and its 3x3 display dilation changes its apparent coverage. The dense mesh uses approximate previews and different sampling, so visual differences are not a controlled improvement measurement. All candidate points, uncertain regions, source data and baseline are retained.

## Decision and next practical repair

The mapping discrepancy is numerically supported, but the new tests do not establish room-wide calibration or pose reliability. Do not train under the conditional gate or introduce a guessed pose correction. A useful repair requires validated, non-repeating static correspondences at multiple depths and regions, then a separately optimized candidate with held-out constraints not used for fitting. If the recording cannot provide these, a new capture with textured static targets and overlap is needed for an independent geometry check; that is a data limitation, not proof that this candidate is wrong. Any repair must retain the current graphs and be evaluated against frozen source identities and fixed views before training.
