## Candidate overlap and revisit checks

- Frames 000110 (36.978 s) and 000129 (43.019 s) show the same far room, with substantial viewpoint change. The far doorway, wall and display boundaries are potential static correspondences; the visitor should be excluded. This is an overlap candidate, not a verified loop constraint.
- Frames 000089 (29.969 s) and 000183 (61.307 s) show the same diorama area from different views roughly 31 seconds apart. The exhibit/backdrop and surrounding architecture could support a distributed revisit test if enough reliable static features and depth exist. Different viewing angles and nearby dynamic objects make this a candidate only.
- Frames 000204 (68.149 s) and 000249 (83.033 s) show the same diorama at different ranges; 000290 and 000296 show it from the side. These may supply additional overlapping observations, but their relative transforms and correspondence quality have not been tested.
- The selected stills do not establish whether any pair closes a trajectory loop or whether surfaces disagree in 3D. A feature-matching and independent geometric check is required before changing the graph.

## Status and next work

The inventory identifies concrete investigation targets, not verified pose or depth failures. Retrieve or reconstruct **dense, unfilled aligned depth** for a small sample of static edges at near, medium and far distances, and during turns; create RGB-depth boundary overlays and measure correspondence error while accounting for depth discontinuities and the RGB-depth time gap. Review additional original DB3 frames near 61 s and the proposed revisits, since 3 FPS neighbors may miss useful observations. Reserve independent frames for validation. The RGB-depth alignment gate is **not yet passed**; no point removal, trajectory repair or training is justified by this inventory alone. Existing recording, data and model were not modified.
